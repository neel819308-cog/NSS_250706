# Cognizant (NASDAQ: CTSH) Business Model Analysis

## Bottom Line
Cognizant is a global professional services company that provides digital transformation, technology consulting, and business process outsourcing services across multiple industries. The company's core customer job-to-be-done is helping enterprises modernize their technology infrastructure and business processes to compete in the digital economy. The profit engine operates through a time-and-materials consulting model combined with long-term outsourcing contracts, leveraging a global delivery network with significant offshore capabilities to maintain competitive pricing while delivering specialized expertise.

## 1. One-paragraph overview
Cognizant is a multinational technology consulting and professional services company that helps organizations transform their business through digital technology solutions. The company serves clients across industries including financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, and communications/media/technology, providing services such as digital strategy consulting, application development, cloud migration, data analytics, AI/ML implementation, and business process outsourcing. Cognizant's core customer job-to-be-done is enabling enterprises to modernize legacy systems, implement new digital capabilities, and optimize business processes to improve efficiency and customer experience. The profit engine operates through a combination of high-margin consulting services and scalable offshore delivery capabilities, with revenue primarily driven by billable hours and long-term service contracts.

## 2. Business model map

**Value proposition**: Digital transformation partner that combines deep industry expertise with global delivery capabilities to help enterprises modernize technology and business processes.

**Key products/services**:
- Digital strategy and consulting
- Application development and modernization
- Cloud infrastructure and migration
- Data analytics and AI/ML solutions
- Business process outsourcing
- Industry-specific technology solutions

**Target customers**: Large enterprises across financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, communications/media/technology, and other industries.

**Distribution**: Direct sales force targeting enterprise clients, with delivery centers globally distributed including significant offshore presence in India.

**Monetization mechanisms**:
- Time-and-materials consulting fees
- Fixed-price project contracts
- Long-term outsourcing agreements
- Managed services contracts
- Software licensing and implementation fees

**Cost structure**:
- **Variable costs**: Employee compensation (consultants, developers, analysts), subcontractor costs, travel expenses
- **Fixed costs**: Global delivery center infrastructure, sales and marketing, research and development, administrative overhead

## 10. Risks and sensitivities

**Top 5 business model risks**:
1. **Talent competition**: Intense competition for skilled consultants and developers
2. **Client concentration**: Dependence on large enterprise clients
3. **Offshore delivery risks**: Political, economic, and operational risks in offshore locations
4. **Technology disruption**: Rapid changes in technology requiring continuous investment
5. **Economic sensitivity**: Consulting spending is highly correlated with economic conditions

**Sensitivity analysis**: 
- **Utilization rate ±5%**: Could impact revenue by ±$200-400M annually
- **Client retention ±5%**: Could impact revenue by ±$300-500M annually
- **Offshore mix ±10%**: Could impact margins by ±100-200 bps

## 11. What would change the thesis

**Positive triggers**:
1. **AI/ML adoption acceleration**: Increased demand for AI implementation services
2. **Cloud migration wave**: Large-scale enterprise cloud transformation projects
3. **Digital transformation spending**: Increased enterprise investment in digital capabilities

**Negative triggers**:
1. **Economic recession**: Reduced enterprise technology spending
2. **Offshore restrictions**: Limitations on visa programs or offshore delivery
3. **Technology commoditization**: Increased competition from automated solutions
